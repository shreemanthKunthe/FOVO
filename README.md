# FOVO — Your Seat. Your View.

**What if you could experience your cinema seat before booking it?**

## The Problem We're Solving

We've all been there. You book a movie ticket, choose what looks like the perfect seat on a tiny seating chart, reach the theatre, and realise the view isn't quite what you expected.

Traditional ticket-booking platforms show you where your seat is, but never what you'll actually see from it.

Choosing a seat for a three-hour movie shouldn't feel like a gamble.

## Why I Started FOVO

I've always wondered why we can preview hotel rooms, explore properties virtually, and try products before buying them, but when it comes to spending hours watching a movie, we're still expected to choose a seat based on a flat 2D map.

That got me thinking: **What if we could actually sit in our chosen seat before booking it?**

That's the idea behind FOVO.

## What FOVO Does

FOVO transforms traditional cinema seating charts into an interactive, first-person viewing experience.

- Explore cinemas, movies and showtimes.
- Browse interactive auditorium seating layouts.
- Select a seat and experience its actual viewing perspective.
- Watch the movie trailer directly on the virtual theatre screen.
- Switch between seats to compare viewing angles before making a decision.

## The Experience

Instead of guessing whether a seat is good, you get to see the screen from that seat's perspective.

**Pick a movie. Choose your seat. Experience the view.**

## Starting Small, Thinking Bigger

FOVO is starting with Hubballi, Karnataka, as its initial market. The goal is to build accurate digital representations of cinema auditoriums and eventually make this experience accessible across theatres and cities.

## The Vision

I don't want people to simply book a seat. I want them to know what they're booking.

**FOVO — Your seat. Your view.**

*Built as an experiment in rethinking an everyday movie-going experience.*
